Why We Need You

High school Speech & Debate tournaments rely completely on volunteer adult judges to function. Tournaments charge steep penalty fees if a school doesn't meet its required judge quota.

Having parent volunteers directly keeps club costs low and ensures every student has the opportunity to compete. No prior debate experience is required!

What to Expect

  • Your Role: Listen attentively to student speeches or debates, keep track of time (timers provided on your phone), and submit scores.
  • The Platform: All scoring and balloting is done digitally via Tabroom.com.
  • The Perks: Enjoy our complimentary hospitality lounge featuring full meals, snacks, and coffee throughout the day!

Interested in Judging?

Get in Touch

Becoming a judge is simple! Here is what the process entails:

  • Reach Out: Send an email to let us know your general availability for upcoming tournament dates.
  • Quick Registration: Set up a free account on Tabroom.com so ballots and round assignments can be linked to you.
  • Brief Orientation: Review a quick 10-minute guide or video on how to fill out a digital ballot (we are also happy to walk you through it live on tournament day!).
  • Show Up & Support: Arrive at the tournament, enjoy the complimentary hospitality lounge snacks, and help our students compete!

Ready to learn more? Contact Mrs. Galaher at gallaherc@fortmillschools.org to get started!
