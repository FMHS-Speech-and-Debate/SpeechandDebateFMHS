Why We Need You

High school Speech & Debate tournaments rely completely on volunteer adult judges to function. Tournaments charge steep penalty fees if a school doesn't meet its required judge quota.

Having parent volunteers directly keeps club costs low and ensures every student has the opportunity to compete. No prior debate experience is required!

What to Expect

  • Your Role: Listen attentively to student speeches or debates, keep track of time (timers provided on your phone), and submit scores.
  • The Platform: All scoring and balloting is done digitally via Tabroom.com.
  • The Perks: Enjoy our complimentary hospitality lounge featuring full meals, snacks, and coffee throughout the day!
