Debate Events

1v1 Value Debate

Lincoln-Douglas Debate (LD)

A structured one-on-one debate focused heavily on social, moral, or philosophical issues. You debate both the Affirmative and Negative sides using a framework of values and criteria[cite: 1].

Format: 1 vs 1 | Time: ~45 min total
2v2 Partner Debate

Public Forum Debate (PF)

A two-on-two team debate focused on current events and timely, real-world issues. Resolutions change throughout the season, requiring solid teamwork, evidence, and quick crossfire thinking[cite: 1].

Format: 2 vs 2 | Focus: Current Events
Legislative Debate

Congressional Debate

Simulate actual lawmakers by debating fake bills, giving short speeches, and grilling other students with questions in a chamber assembly. Loud, fast, and great for leadership[cite: 1].

Format: Chamber Assembly | Prep: Docket-based
Team Debate Style

World Schools Debate (WSD)

Combines dynamic styles of impromptu and prepared motion debating. Teams engage in lively, conversational yet rigorous exchanges on global issues[cite: 1].

Format: Team Format | Focus: Global & Motion-based

Public Address Events

Limited Prep

Extemporaneous Speaking

Students are given 3 topics to choose from about current world issues, have 30 minutes to prepare a speech with evidence, and 7 minutes to deliver it without notes[cite: 1].

Prep: 30 Minutes | Speech: 7 Minutes
Original Persuasion

Original Oratory (OO)

A 10-minute persuasive speech written entirely by the student. Persuades the audience about a significant topic using logic, evidence, clear organization, and emotional appeal[cite: 1].

Format: Memorized | Time Limit: 10 Minutes
Informative Speech

Informative Speaking

Students choose a worthwhile topic to inform and teach an audience something new without being persuasive[cite: 1]. Can include visual or auditory aids and requires a bibliography.

Format: Memorized | Time Limit: 10 Minutes
Informative Research

Expository

An informative speech written by the student that uses research to educate the audience about a topic without visual aids[cite: 1]. Focuses on relevance, clarity, and organization.

Format: Memorized | Time Limit: 5 Minutes
Delivered Speech

Declamation

Delivering a speech written and previously performed by someone else in an impassioned way. You use public speaking skills and your own interpretation to give the speech new meaning[cite: 1].

Format: Speech Delivery | Time Limit: 10 Minutes
Limited Prep

Impromptu Speaking

Given 3 prompts to choose from (typically famous quotes), you have 7 minutes total for choosing, planning, and speaking conversationally backed by 2–3 evidence examples[cite: 1].

Format: 7-Min Total (Prep + Speech) | Focus: On-the-Spot

Interpretation Events

Acting / Drama

Dramatic Interpretation (DI)

Present a cutting from published plays, screenplays, or fictional/non-fictional works that are dramatic in nature. Features deep character commitment and emotion[cite: 1].

Format: Solo Performance | Time Limit: 10 Minutes
Acting / Comedy

Humorous Interpretation (HI)

Similar to dramatic interpretation, but the intent is to entertain and be funny. Students select a work and perform it humorously, taking on multiple character personalities[cite: 1].

Format: Solo Performance | Time Limit: 10 Minutes
Partner Performance

Duo Interpretation (DUO)

Performed with a partner where you select a piece and perform it together portraying multiple characters[cite: 1]. Partners may not look at or touch each other during the performance.

Format: 2 Performers | Time Limit: 10 Minutes
Literature Reading

Poetry and Prose Interpretation

Focuses on bringing published poetry or prose to life through your voice and interpretation using a manuscript binder[cite: 1]. Poetry emphasizes rhythm and emotion; prose focuses on storytelling.

Format: Manuscript Piece | Time Limit: 5 Minutes
Multi-Genre Performance

Program Oral Interpretation (POI)

A 10-minute performance combining prose, poetry, and/or drama around one central theme[cite: 1]. Must use at least 2 of the 3 genres with a manuscript binder.

Format: Multi-Genre Solo | Time Limit: 10 Minutes
